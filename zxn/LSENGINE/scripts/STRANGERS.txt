# STRANGERS - nameless people in the street; HINT - a stranger who knows who you're after
=== STR01
- THEY ARE ON THE PHONE. THEY ARE ALWAYS ON THE PHONE. THEY WILL DIE ON THE PHONE.
=== STR02
- EARBUDS IN. THEY NOD TO A BEAT YOU CAN'T HEAR AND WALK STRAIGHT THROUGH YOUR SENTENCE.
=== STR03
- "SORRY, LATE, LATE, LATE!" THEY RUN FOR THE BUS. THE BUS LEAVES. THEY RUN FOR THE NEXT ONE.
=== STR04
- THEY ARE TAKING A SELFIE. YOU ARE NOW IN THE BACKGROUND OF FORTY SELFIES.
- "CAN YOU MOVE? NO - STAY. YOU LOOK AUTHENTIC."
=== STR05
- AN AJUSSHI IS PRACTISING HIS GOLF SWING WITH AN UMBRELLA.
- HE LOOKS AT YOU. HE LOOKS AT THE HORIZON. "FORE," HE SAYS, AND SWINGS.
=== STR06
- A STUDENT WITH FLASHCARDS. "ABANDON. ABDUCT. ABHOR." THEY DON'T LOOK UP.
- THEY'RE STILL ON THE A'S.
=== STR07
- A TOURIST WITH A PAPER MAP STOPS YOU FIRST. "EXCUSE ME, WHERE IS SEOUL?"
- YOU POINT AT EVERYTHING.
=== STR08
- A DELIVERY RIDER SCREECHES TO A STOP. "PALLI PALLI!" HE SAYS, TO NOBODY, AND IS GONE.
- YOUR HAIR IS STILL BLOWING.
=== STR09
- A HALMEONI LOOKS YOU UP AND DOWN. "TOO THIN. EAT." SHE PRESSES A TANGERINE INTO YOUR HAND.
!ENERGY 3
- YOU DIDN'T EVEN SAY ANNYEONGHASEYO.
=== STR10
- THEY ARE WATCHING A DRAMA ON THEIR PHONE. SOMEONE ON SCREEN HAS JUST BEEN SLAPPED WITH A FISH.
- "NOT NOW. NOT NOW!"
=== STR11
- A MAN IN A SUIT IS ASLEEP STANDING UP. HE WAKES, SAYS "YES, SIR," AND FALLS ASLEEP AGAIN.
=== STR12
- A SCHOOLKID IN A HUGE PADDED COAT WADDLES PAST LIKE A SLEEPING BAG ON A MISSION.
=== STR13
- "ARE YOU SELLING SOMETHING?" "NO, I'M THE POSTIE." "THAT'S WORSE." THEY LEAVE.
=== STR14
- AN AJUMMA IN A VISOR POWER-WALKS PAST, ARMS PUMPING. YOU TRY TO KEEP UP. YOU CANNOT KEEP UP.
=== STR15
- THEY ARE ARGUING WITH A VENDING MACHINE. THE VENDING MACHINE IS WINNING.
=== STR16
- A COUPLE IN MATCHING OUTFITS. MATCHING HATS. MATCHING SHOES. THEY STARE AT YOU IN STEREO.
=== STR17
- A STREAMER IS LIVE. "SAY HI TO MY FOUR VIEWERS!" YOU WAVE. "THREE. ONE LEFT."
=== STR18
- A JOGGER RUNS ON THE SPOT AT THE LIGHTS. "CAN'T STOP. CAN'T STOP." THE LIGHTS ARE GREEN.
- SHE STILL DOESN'T STOP.
=== STR19
- A MAN IS FEEDING PIGEONS. THE PIGEONS LOOK AT YOU. THE MAN LOOKS AT YOU. NOBODY SPEAKS.
=== STR20
- "ANNYEONG-" YOU BEGIN. THEY BOW, BACK AWAY AND BOW AGAIN UNTIL THEY'RE ROUND THE CORNER.
=== STR21
- A WOMAN IS TALKING TO HER DOG IN A PRAM. THE DOG HAS SUNGLASSES. THE DOG IGNORES YOU TOO.
=== STR22
- AN OFFICE WORKER CHEWS A KIMBAP ROLL WHILE TYPING ON TWO PHONES. "EMAIL ME."
=== STR23
- A STUDENT ASKS IF YOU'LL FILL IN A SURVEY. IT'S FORTY PAGES. YOU ESCAPE AT PAGE THREE.
=== STR24
- A TEENAGER IS PRACTISING A DANCE IN A SHOP WINDOW'S REFLECTION. SHE SPINS. YOU SPIN. NO.
=== STR25
- AN AJUSSHI ON A BENCH POINTS AT YOUR BAG. "IN MY DAY WE CARRIED THAT UP THE HILL. BOTH WAYS."
=== STR26
- THEY ARE WEARING A FACE MASK, A HAT, SUNGLASSES AND A HOOD. IT COULD BE ANYONE. IT IGNORES YOU.
=== STR27
- A LOST TOURIST ASKS YOU THE WAY TO N SEOUL TOWER. YOU POINT UP. THEY SIGH AT THE HILL.
=== STR28
- A MAN IS QUEUEING FOR A NEW BAKERY. THE QUEUE HAS BEEN THERE SINCE TUESDAY. "DON'T PUSH IN."
=== STR29
- A KID ON A SCOOTER SHOUTS "POSTIE!" AND SALUTES. YOU SALUTE BACK. HE'S ALREADY GONE.
=== STR30
- A STALL AJUMMA HANDS YOU A FREE HOTTEOK. "YOU WALK TOO MUCH. SIT. EAT. GO."
!ENERGY 3
=== HINT01
- "{T}? OH, THEY LIVE IN {D}. LOVELY. TERRIBLE SINGER. THE WHOLE STREET HEARS THE SHOWER."
=== HINT02
- "{T}? IN {D}, NEAR THE TOP OF THE HILL. ALWAYS LATE FOR EVERYTHING. EVEN BIRTHDAYS. THEIR OWN."
=== HINT03
- "{T}! EVERYBODY KNOWS {T}. {D}. ORDERS CHICKEN EVERY NIGHT. THE DELIVERY RIDER HAS A KEY."
=== HINT04
- "{T}? TRY {D}. BIG FAN OF THE HAN RIVER. SMALL FAN OF RENT. DON'T MENTION THE EX."
