# THINGS - the things on the round (mostly the narrator)
=== VENDING
- A VENDING MACHINE HUMS ON THE CORNER. COFFEE IN CANS, HOT OR COLD. IT HAS SEEN THINGS.
? A can of coffee
  $-1
  !ENERGY 5
  !SPEED 10

? Walk on
  - THE MACHINE HUMS SADLY. YOU'LL BE BACK. EVERYONE COMES BACK.
=== POSTBOX
- A RED POSTBOX. ONE OF YOURS. YOU PAT IT. IT'S WARM FROM THE SUN.
- SOMEBODY'S STUCK A STICKER ON IT: A CAT IN A HAT. YOU LEAVE IT. THE CAT LOOKS GOOD.
=== BINOCS
- YOU LOOK THROUGH THE BINOCULARS AT THE TOP OF {PLACE}.
- NORTH: BUKHANSAN, GREY GRANITE PEAKS LIKE A GIANT'S KNUCKLES. BELOW THEM, THE PALACE ROOFS.
- SOUTH: THE HAN, WIDE AND SLOW, AND BRIDGE AFTER BRIDGE, STITCHING THE CITY TOGETHER.
- SOMEWHERE DOWN THERE IS A LIFT THAT ISN'T COMING. YOU CAN'T SEE IT. YOU CAN FEEL IT.
=== EXHIBIT
- A GLASS CASE. INSIDE: A GOLD CROWN OF SILLA, FROM A TOMB IN GYEONGJU. FIFTEEN HUNDRED YEARS OLD.
- ITS UPRIGHTS ARE SHAPED LIKE TREES AND DEER ANTLERS, HUNG WITH GOLD SPANGLES AND COMMA-SHAPED JADE.
- THE GOLD IS SO THIN THAT SOME THINK IT WAS MADE FOR THE TOMB, NOT THE HEAD. STILL NO ADDRESS ON IT.
=== HIT
- BEEP! A DELIVERY SCOOTER SWINGS ROUND THE CORNER ON THE PAVEMENT, WHERE SCOOTERS LIVE NOW.
- YOU SPIN. THE LETTERS SPIN. THE SKY SPINS. A BOX OF FRIED CHICKEN SPINS PAST.
- YOU SIT ON THE KERB FOR A WHILE, COUNTING STARS. THERE ARE ELEVEN. THE CLOCK KEEPS GOING.
=== TIRED
- YOUR LEGS STOP. NOT YOU. YOUR LEGS. THEY'VE DECIDED.
- YOU SIT ON A KERB. A PIGEON SITS NEXT TO YOU. NEITHER OF YOU HAS EATEN. YOU NEED FOOD.
=== HUNGRY
- YOUR STOMACH MAKES A NOISE LIKE A TRAIN PULLING IN. AN AJUMMA HEARS IT AND TUTS.
- 'EAT SOMETHING,' SHE SAYS. KIMBAP. TTEOKBOKKI. ANYTHING. THE BUBBLE WRAP IS STARTING TO LOOK GOOD.
=== NOMONEY
- YOU DIG IN YOUR POCKETS. A RECEIPT. A BUTTON. A RUBBER BAND FROM THE SORTING ROOM.
- NOT ENOUGH WON. YOU SMILE, BOW, AND BACK OUT SLOWLY.
=== LIFT
- YOU PRESS THE BUTTON. THE LIFT IS ON 23. IT STAYS ON 23. IT IS HAPPY ON 23.
- IT COMES DOWN, STOPPING ON EVERY FLOOR. NOBODY GETS ON. NOBODY GETS OFF. IT'S SIGHTSEEING.
- DING. A MAN, A DOG AND A BICYCLE GET OUT. THERE'S ROOM FOR YOU AND HALF A PARCEL.
=== STAIRS
- THE VILLA STAIRS. NARROW, STEEP, TILED, AND SLIPPERY IF ANYONE EVEN THINKS ABOUT RAIN.
- A SHOE RACK ON EVERY LANDING. A POT PLANT ON EVERY STEP. A DOG BEHIND EVERY DOOR.
- YOU REACH THE TOP. YOU AND YOUR KNEES NEED A MINUTE.
=== TRAIN
- A CHIME, A CALM VOICE, AND THE PLATFORM SCREEN DOORS SLIDE OPEN. YOU SQUEEZE IN.
- EVERYONE IS ON A PHONE. A DRAMA, A GAME, A SHARE PRICE GOING DOWN. NOBODY LOOKS UP.
- THE SEATS AT THE END OF THE CAR ARE FOR THE ELDERLY. NOBODY YOUNG SITS THERE. NOT EVEN YOU, WITH YOUR LEGS.
- AT THE TRANSFER STATION A LITTLE TRADITIONAL TUNE PLAYS. CHANGING LINES FEELS LIKE A FESTIVAL.
=== BRIDGE
- THE BRIDGE IS LONGER THAN IT LOOKED ON THE MAP. EVERYTHING IS.
- THE WIND OFF THE HAN TRIES TO TAKE YOUR LETTERS. YOU HUG THE BAG LIKE IT OWES YOU MONEY.
- BELOW, A KILOMETRE OF SLOW GREEN RIVER. CARS ROAR PAST. A CYCLIST WAVES. YOU KEEP WALKING.
=== RIVER
- THE HAN RIVER PARK. BIKES WHIZZ PAST. A KITE. A DOG IN A VISOR.
- FAMILIES IN LITTLE SHADE TENTS. FRIENDS ON MATS WITH CHIMAEK - CHICKEN AND BEER, DELIVERED TO THE GRASS.
- THE CHICKEN RIDER IS HAVING A BETTER DAY THAN YOU. THEY'VE GOT A SCOOTER.
=== CLOSED
- THE SHUTTER IS DOWN. A SIGN SAYS BACK IN 10 MINUTES. IT DOESN'T SAY WHICH 10 MINUTES.
=== NIGHT
- 18:00. THE ROUND IS OVER. THE NEON COMES ON, ONE SIGN AT A TIME.
- YOU WALK BACK TO MYEONGDONG WITH WHATEVER'S LEFT IN YOUR BAG. YOUR FEET HAVE WRITTEN YOU A LETTER. IT'S MOSTLY SWEARING.
